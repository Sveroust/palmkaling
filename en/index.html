Arenga pinnata

What we ship

Kolang-kaling is the immature endosperm of the sugar palm, sold in Southeast Asia as attap chee and used in desserts, canned fruit cocktails, and beverages. It arrives boiled and held in brine or light syrup, translucent and firm, ready for your own processing line.

Where it comes from

Sugar palm is not planted in blocks like oil palm. It grows scattered across smallholder land on hill slopes, which is why consolidation — not plantation size — is what determines whether an export volume can actually be filled.

Why buyers work with us

Graded before it is packed
