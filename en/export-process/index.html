Export Process

How an order runs

From first enquiry to bill of lading, this is the sequence and roughly what each step costs in time.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You send grade, volume, packing, and destination port. We return an indicative price, normally within one working day.

    1 day
  2. 02

    Sample

    A 1 kg sample ships free of charge; you cover the courier. Larger evaluation samples are quoted separately.

    5–10 days
  3. 03

    Contract and PI

    We issue a proforma invoice with the agreed specification, Incoterm, and payment terms. Advance payment starts production.

    2–3 days
  4. 04

    Production

    Fruit is collected, boiled, graded, and packed against your lot. This is the step that sets the lead time.

    14–20 days
  5. 05

    Inspection and stuffing

    Optional third-party inspection, then the container is stuffed and sealed at the processing site.

    1–2 days
  6. 06

    Documents

    Invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary and health certificates are issued and sent by courier and email.

    3–5 days
  7. 07

    Shipment

    Loaded at Tanjung Priok. Transit time depends on your port and carrier.

    varies
