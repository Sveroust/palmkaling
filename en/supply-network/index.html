Quality control

Five checkpoints between the tree and the container. Each one is a point where a lot can be rejected, not merely graded down.

  1. 01

    Incoming sorting

    Fruit arriving from collectors is inspected for maturity and browning. Over-ripe lots are rejected at intake, not blended in.

  2. 02

    Boiling and soaking

    Boiled to remove the irritant sap, then soaked and washed in changes of water until the residual astringency is gone.

  3. 03

    Diameter grading

    Separated into Grade A and Grade B by size, so each container matches the grade on the contract.

  4. 04

    Brining and packing

    Filled, brine or syrup added to specification, sealed, and coded with the lot number that follows it onto the packing list.

  5. 05

    Pre-shipment inspection

    Buyer or third-party inspection (SGS, Sucofindo) can be arranged before stuffing, at buyer's cost.

Traceability

Every pail carries a lot code that identifies the harvest area and the boiling batch. If a claim is raised, the affected lot can be isolated instead of the whole shipment.
